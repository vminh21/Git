### 🚀 About Me

I am an **AI Engineer** focused on **Computer Vision, Document AI (OCR), and Generative AI (LLMs, RAG & Agents)**. I enjoy taking models out of notebooks and turning them into working products — clean pipelines, fast APIs, and simple UIs that real users can use. My background in full-stack web development helps me ship AI end-to-end, from data to deployment.

- 👁️ **Computer Vision & Document AI**: Built a 2-stage **YOLOv8** pipeline for Vietnamese ID card (CCCD) recognition — card detection & cropping, field localization, then OCR with **VietOCR / EasyOCR**, served via **FastAPI** and **Gradio**.
- 🧠 **LLMs, RAG & Agents**: Hands-on with retrieval & context engineering, data ingestion into vector stores with benchmarking, and **ReAct-style agent loops** with tool use.
- 📊 **Machine Learning Foundations**: Spam classification comparing classical ML, deep learning, embeddings and LLM-based approaches.
- 🛠️ **Production & Full-Stack**: Solid engineering base with **Python, FastAPI, Docker, Node.js, React and MySQL** to deliver AI features inside real applications.
